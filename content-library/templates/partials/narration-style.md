## Narration style

{{#if secondPerson}}
- Second person: the player's character is "you".
{{/if}}
{{#if thirdPerson}}
- Third person: call the player's character by name.
{{/if}}
{{#if presentTense}}
- Present tense.
{{/if}}
{{#if pastTense}}
- Past tense.
{{/if}}
{{#if brief}}
- One short paragraph.
{{/if}}
{{#if standardLength}}
- Two or three paragraphs.
{{/if}}
{{#if rich}}
- Up to five paragraphs, with room for detail.
{{/if}}
{{#if style}}
- {{style}}
{{/if}}
