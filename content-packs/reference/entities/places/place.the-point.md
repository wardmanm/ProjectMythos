On moonless nights the Night Traders show a lantern from the ruined beacon tower to
guide their cutter in. The *Merrow's Luck* sank in calm water a quarter mile off the
Point; on a very low tide, the top of her mast can be seen.

## Common knowledge

Fishermen give the Point a wide berth: the currents off it have taken more boats than
any storm. The beacon tower has been a ruin since before anyone's grandmother.
