The Night Traders hold Varek Holm's gambling debt and take payment in clearances. The
*Merrow's Luck* was carrying forty crates of their brandy when she sank. They pulled
Jory Ashby from the water and are holding him up the coast.

## Common knowledge

Everyone on this coast has heard of smugglers, and most have bought something that
came ashore without paying dues. Nobody admits to knowing who they are.
