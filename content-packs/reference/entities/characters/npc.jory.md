Jory came to Brinemoor in August to clear a debt and signed on with the *Merrow's
Luck* for the extra pay the harbor clerk promised her crew. He did not drown. When
the overloaded boat foundered off the Point, the Night Traders' cutter that was
waiting to meet her pulled him from the water. They are holding him at a shepherd's
bothy up the coast while they decide whether a living witness is worth more as a
hostage or less as a risk.
