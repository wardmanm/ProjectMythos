Pip has no family anyone knows of. She sleeps in the net loft behind the Gull and
Anchor and eats at Odette's back door. On the night the *Merrow's Luck* sailed, she
was on the harbor wall late and saw the harbor clerk hurrying toward the boats with a
shuttered lantern and his leather document case. She has not told anyone, because no
one has asked.

## Common knowledge

A harbor orphan who runs errands and messages for a penny. Odette feeds her.
