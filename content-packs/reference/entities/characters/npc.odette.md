Odette has kept the Gull and Anchor since her husband Bram died. The Wardens fined
Bram for a cargo he swore he never carried, and she has never forgiven them. She
feeds Pip most days without being asked. Jory Ashby lodged with her in August, and
she liked him; she thought he talked too freely about money. She believes Varek's
story about the squall, because Varek has never lied to her that she knows of.

## Common knowledge

The widow who keeps the Gull and Anchor. Knows everyone in Brinemoor and most of
their business. Her fish stew is the best on the coast, and she will tell you so.
