Tomas has fished out of Brinemoor for fifty years and mends nets on the harbor wall
when the weather keeps him ashore. On the night of September 2nd he was there late,
and saw the *Merrow's Luck* slip out with no lanterns lit, riding low in the water.
The sea was flat calm that night and the next. He knows there was no squall, and he
knows boats that sail dark are carrying something. He owes Odette for a winter's
worth of meals, and he does not want trouble.

## Common knowledge

The oldest fisherman still working out of Brinemoor. Mends nets on the harbor wall.
Knows the weather and the water better than anyone, and talks less than anyone.
