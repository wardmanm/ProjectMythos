Wren Ashby keeps the books for a chandler in Hollin, two days inland by coach. She
raised her younger brother Jory after their father drowned and their mother's health
failed, and has been getting him out of trouble ever since. She came to Brinemoor
because two letters sit badly together: Jory's, boasting of night work the harbor
clerk had found him, and the harbor office's notice of his death, signed by that same
clerk.

## Common knowledge

A bookkeeper from Hollin. Her younger brother Jory crewed the *Merrow's Luck*.
